One of the most beautiful sunsets on earth. Walk south toward Petitenget or north toward Double Six. Grab a cold Bintang from a beach vendor and just stand there for a bit.

Dinner — Ocean Grand Beach
Nightly fire ring performance and live band, right on the beach. Excellent seafood — whole sea bass, scallops, crab. Very family-friendly outdoor setting. ★4.8, 2,000+ reviews. No need to book for a group of 8.

Seminyak — Beach Club & Sunset Bars

Seminyak's best coffee, hidden down a tiny alley — worth finding. Excellent eggs, avo toast, smoothie bowls. Go early before the queue builds. Walk there from the villa, ~10 min.

Potato Head Beach Club
Reserve a sun-bed section online — walk-ins turned away on weekends. Built from reclaimed wooden doors, directly on the beach. Drinks, food, pool, sun all day. Stay as long as you like.

Potato Head has decent food if you want to stay put. Or wander up to La Plancha (beachside Mexican, colourful bean bags on the sand) or KU DE TA for a proper sit-down lunch. All within a 10 min walk.

Walk Jl. Seminyak and Jl. Oberoi — great boutiques, local sarong shops, jewellery. Pick up anything you need. The area is flat and easy with strollers.

2-for-1 cocktails at happy hour. Panoramic views over the entire Seminyak coastline. Best group sunset spot on the strip.
7:30 PM
Dinner — KUUKI Rooftop or La Lucciola
KUUKI for Chef Saiful's intimate Itameshi tasting menu (book ahead). La Lucciola for a beautiful beachfront Italian with great fresh pasta — very family-friendly, open air, stroller no problem. Both are within easy reach of the villa.
10:30 PM
Savaya Bali — Late Night (whoever's up for it)
Clifftop club in Uluwatu, ~40 min from Seminyak. World-class DJs, built into the cliff above the Indian Ocean — genuinely spectacular. Book entry or a table in advance. Pre-arrange the driver return.

Monday June 15 – Thursday June 18 · 3 nights
Monday · 15 June
Arrive Ubud — Settle & Culture Night
5:30 PM
Check In — Villa Pangkung Indah
Ubud feels entirely different from Seminyak — cooler (~24°C), quieter, lush green jungle everywhere. Explore the villa, try the pool table, sit on the terrace. The sound of jungle birds is immediate.
7:00 PM
Dinner — Lotus Café Ubud
Beautiful open-air restaurant set over a lotus pond, right opposite the Ubud Palace. Classic Balinese food, stunning setting, very relaxed. Perfect first dinner in Ubud — great for the whole group including the kids.
8:00 PM
Ubud Palace — Kecak or Legong Dance (optional)
Held nightly at the Royal Palace right next to Lotus Café. ~IDR 100k per adult. Sara will love the fire and costumes. Arrive 20 min early for front seats. If the group is tired from travel, skip tonight and catch it another evening — it's on every night.
Tuesday · 16 June
Rice Terraces, Swings & Waterfall
7:30 AM
Tegallalang Rice Terraces
Go early — the light is extraordinary before 9 AM and crowds are thin. The green terraces are spectacular in June. Strollers manage the main paths well. Bring the carrier for Shlok on any narrower tracks.
9:00 AM
Mupu Rice Terrace — Hidden Gem (★4.9)
Just next to Tegallalang — a family-owned plot with a warm, chatty owner who sells huge fresh coconuts and explains the rice growing cycle. No crowds, totally authentic.
10:00 AM
Swing Heaven Ubud (or Bali Swing)
The famous Bali jungle swings over rice fields and the valley. Swing Heaven is the friends' recommendation — great views, multiple swing options, photogenic. Bali Swing (the original) is nearby if Swing Heaven is busy. Book ahead online. Both are ~20 min from Tegallalang.
12:30 PM
Lunch — Taman Dedari
Stunning open-air lunch spot in Ubud — lush tropical gardens, beautiful food, very family-friendly setting. Highly recommended by the friends. Great for a big group. Try the grilled fish and the Balinese satay.
2:30 PM
Ubud Waterfall — Tegenungan or Tibumana
Tegenungan is the closest to Ubud (~15 min) and the most accessible — wide paths, good for strollers near the viewing area. Tibumana is quieter and more beautiful but a steeper walk. Both are free to enter (~IDR 20k parking). Spectacular swimming pools at the base.
5:00 PM
Ubud Local Market & Jl. Hanoman Browse
Ubud Art Market (Pasar Seni) on Jl. Raya Ubud is the main market — batik, sarongs, silver, carved wood. Bargain hard (start at 30–40% of asking price). Also browse Jl. Hanoman for silver jewellery and art galleries. Pick up sarongs here for the temple visits tomorrow.
7:30 PM
Dinner — Locavore or Hujan Locale
Locavore is one of SE Asia's finest restaurants — book weeks ahead. Adults rotate in two sittings of 4. Hujan Locale is the excellent group-friendly fallback. Both on Jl. Dewi Sita, central Ubud.
Wednesday · 17 June
Temples, Water & Slow Afternoon
8:30 AM
Tirta Empul — Sacred Water Temple
Bali's most sacred spring-fed purification pool. Sarong required at the gate (provided free). Sara is old enough to understand "holy water" and will remember this. Genuinely moving to witness. ~30 min from the villa.
10:30 AM
Goa Gajah — Elephant Cave Temple
9th-century rock-carved temple with a famous demon-face entrance. Kids love the carved stone figure. A 20 min drive from Tirta Empul. Quick stop, ~45 min total. Sarong on.
12:30 PM
Lunch — Taman Dedari or Sari Organik
Taman Dedari for a lush garden setting with excellent food (highly recommended). Sari Organik if you want to walk through rice fields to reach it. Both work brilliantly for a midday group stop.
2:30 PM

Karsa Spa has a children's play area — adults can fully relax. Book all 6 slots simultaneously. Traditional Balinese massage ~IDR 250k / 90 min. Or do in-villa massage if the group prefers staying put.
5:30 PM
Campuhan Ridge Walk — Golden Hour
Free 2km walk along a jungle ridge with sweeping views over two rivers. Best at golden hour. Stroller-friendly on the main paved path. Ends near several good dinner spots in central Ubud.
7:30 PM

Tips & Essentials
👶
Travelling with Shlok (16 months)
You have strollers — use them. Most Ubud streets, the main waterfall viewpoint, Tegallalang paths, and Seminyak beach are all stroller-friendly. Bring a soft structured carrier as backup for temples (uneven stone steps) and anywhere with stairs. Pack familiar snacks and a tablet for long drives. Night flights: prepare sensory toys for the overnight KTM→KUL leg.
🧒
Travelling with Sara (3 years)
Sara is at a perfect age for Bali. She'll remember the monkeys, fire dancers, temple carvings, and pool forever. Buy her a small sarong at Jl. Hanoman — kids love wearing them at temples. The Ubud Palace dance is magical for this age. Keep a change of clothes accessible in the day bag — she will find both water and mud.
🏊
Pool Safety — Non-Negotiable
Always request a pool fence or safety net before arrival at both villas. With Shlok (16 months) this is non-negotiable. Most quality villas provide this at no charge on request. State it in your booking message, your confirmation email, and once more by WhatsApp 2 days before arrival. Don't assume it will be there.
🌡️
June Weather — Ideal Timing
Early dry season — the best time of year to visit Bali. Days 28–31°C, low humidity, almost no rain, cool Ubud evenings (~22°C). Apply SPF 50+ every 90 min on the kids — tropical sun is deceptive even when cloudy. Ubud mornings at the rice terraces can be misty and cool — bring a light layer.
🚐
Group Transport
Book a dedicated 12-seater minibus for all major transfers: DPS airport arrival (Jun 13), Seminyak→Ubud (Jun 15), and Ubud→DPS (Jun 18). ~IDR 600–900k per trip, split 6 ways = almost nothing. Have the villa arrange it — they know trusted drivers. Never rely on multiple Grab rides with 2 toddlers and a mountain of luggage.
🛕
Temple Etiquette
All visitors must wear a sarong below the knee (provided or sold at gates, ~IDR 30–50k). Speak quietly inside. Never step over temple offerings on the ground — step around them. Avoid pointing with one finger; use the whole hand. Children can enter all the temples on this itinerary. A genuinely respectful attitude will earn smiles from local worshippers.
🇦🇺
Sitasma & Ajnish (Australia)
Direct flights to Bali from Sydney ~6h, Melbourne ~6.5h, Brisbane ~7h. Confirm DPS arrival time immediately so you can coordinate the airport minibus. Australian citizens need a Bali Visa on Arrival (VOA) — USD 35 per person at DPS airport. Have USD cash ready or use the airport VOA card payment machine.
💊
Health & Medicine
Pack one shared kit: DEET mosquito repellent (especially for evenings), oral rehydration salts for the kids, antihistamine, paracetamol for toddlers, sunburn cream, baby formula/food for Shlok if required. Avoid ice from street stalls, only bottled water. Good pharmacies (Apotek) are widely available. Bali belly is possible — go easy on the first day.
💸
Money & Cash
Cash is king in Bali. Withdraw IDR at DPS airport ATMs on arrival — competitive rates and always available. Avoid street money changers (scam risk). Use official changers at Seminyak Square or your villa. Approximate: IDR 100,000 ≈ AUD 10 / USD 6. Good restaurant meal: IDR 100–200k per person. Bali massage: IDR 150–250k per hour.
📱
Connectivity & Coordination
Buy a Telkomsel SIM at the DPS airport (best coverage island-wide, ~IDR 100k for 10–15GB). Create a WhatsApp group for all 6 adults before you land. Share villa addresses and the driver's WhatsApp in the group. Download Bali maps offline on Google Maps before you fly — some areas have patchy signal.
🙏
Cultural Respect
Bali is predominantly Hindu. Step around offerings (small flower baskets on the ground) — never over them. A simple "Selamat pagi" (good morning) or "Terima kasih" (thank you) earns genuine warmth everywhere. Tipping is not mandatory but deeply appreciated — IDR 20–50k for good service feels right.
🎒
Packing Smart for the Group
Pack one shared first-aid kit. Reef-safe sunscreen. A portable bluetooth speaker for villa evenings. Light linen shirts/dresses for restaurants. Laundry at the villa is ~IDR 15,000/kg. Pack light — you'll buy things in Seminyak you didn't know you needed.
👶
Don't Forget to Pack
Clip-on portable fan — essential for the stroller and for Shlok in the Bali heat. Game changer.

Baby monitor / camera — for when the kids are asleep in the villa and adults are out by the pool or at dinner.

Noise-cancelling headphones — for the overnight KTM→KUL flight and the layover. Non-negotiable for sanity.
🎉
Savaya Bali — Late Night
One of Bali's most spectacular clubs — carved into a clifftop in Uluwatu with views over the Indian Ocean. World-class DJs, extraordinary setting, unforgettable. ~40 min from Seminyak. Best on Jun 14 (Saturday night). Book table/entry in advance — it sells out. Dress code: smart. Sort a driver for the return — don't Grab from there late night.
